Methods · Places are assigned by point-in-polygon; offshore quakes go to the nearest country within 250 km (US states within 200 km). Sequences: Gardner & Knopoff (1974) windows at the local completeness magnitude. b-value: Aki–Utsu maximum likelihood above the magnitude of completeness (maximum curvature + 0.2). Energy: log₁₀E = 1.5M + 4.8 J; Hiroshima ≈ 6.3×10¹³ J.
